NVOCC (Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier) is a licensed ocean transportation intermediary that issues its own bills of lading without owning vessels. Oceanus Line operates as a specialized NVOCC, offering end-to-end container logistics, flexible scheduling, and custom clearance for liquid bulk and standard dry cargo across global shipping lanes.
ISO tank containers are specially built stainless steel pressure vessels designed to safely transport liquid bulk cargo. We handle hazardous and non-hazardous chemicals, liquid foodstuffs, petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, gases, and industrial oils in strict compliance with IMDG and international ISO standards.
We provide tailored liquid bulk and multimodal logistics solutions to major industry sectors, including Chemical Manufacturing, Food & Beverage Processing, Pharmaceuticals, Gas & Oil Operations, Atomic Energy Infrastructure, and Wood Processing.
Safety is our top priority. We operate with certified hazmat handlers, full IMDG code compliance, rigorous pre-trip tank testing & cleaning inspections, 24/7 real-time GPS & telemetry tracking, and comprehensive risk mitigation protocols at every stage of transit.
Our expansive global logistics network covers major trade corridors spanning the Middle East, Indian Subcontinent, Southeast Asia, Europe, Africa, and North & South America with full intermodal connectivity across ocean, rail, and road.
