Standard Tanks

Standard Tanks Built For Bulk Liquid Cargo

Standard Tanks are T11 tank containers designed for the transportation of food grade, hazardous and non-hazardous cargoes, available in a wide range of capacities and can also be used as statutory storage tanks.

Quick Response

Timely delivery and a commitment to maintain the integrity of shipments, backed by an expert team at every stage.

  • What cargoes can your ISO tanks carry?
    Our T11 standard tanks are designed for the transportation of food grade, hazardous and non-hazardous cargoes, including chemicals, food-grade liquids, industrial liquids and other specialized products.
  • How large is your ISO tank fleet?
    We operate close to 850 ISO tank containers in the Middle East, Indian Subcontinent, South East Asia, Far East, Africa, Europe and Red Sea ports and other areas on a case to case basis. We will expand further by adding more tanks so that by the end of this year we operate at least 1000+ ISO tanks.
  • What are the specifications of your standard tanks?
    External Length 20ft (6,058 mm), External Width 8ft (2,438 mm), External Height 8ft 6in (2,591 mm), MAWP up to 4.0 bar, Capacity 26,000 ltr and Tare 3,540 - 3,830 kg. They are available in a wide range of capacities and can also be used as statutory storage tanks.
  • Which core services do you offer?
    Our core services are NVOCC, ISO Tank Operator, Freight Forwarding, Project Cargo, Supply Chain Services and Agency Representation.
  • How does your operational flow work?
    Depot out, cleaning, filling, customs export, sailed from origin, arrived at destination, customs import, delivery and depot in.
  • Where does your network reach?
    Corporate Office: UAE (Dubai). Network locations — China: Shanghai, Shenzhen, Tianjin, Qingdao, Wuhan. Japan: Osaka, Nagoya, Yokohama, Kobe. Korea: Busan, Kwangyang, Incheon. Taiwan: Keelung, Kaohsiung, Taichung. Philippines: Manila, Batangas. Vietnam: Ho Chi Minh, Haiphong. Mediterranean Ports: Alexandria, Evyap, Gebze, Haifa, Ashdod.
