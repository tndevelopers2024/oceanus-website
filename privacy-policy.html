Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Oceanus Line & Container Logistics LLC collects through this website, why we collect it and what we do with it. It applies to this website only.

Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form or the quote request form on this website, we collect the details you enter – typically your name, email address, telephone number, company name and the details of the shipment or service you are enquiring about.

We do not ask for payment card details, identity document numbers or any other financial information through this website.

How We Use It

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry, to prepare a quotation, and to provide the logistics services you ask us about. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who We Share It With

Where it is necessary to carry out the service you have requested, we may share relevant shipment details with carriers, port agents, depots and customs authorities involved in your consignment. We share only what is needed to move and clear the cargo.

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by applicable law or by a competent authority.

Retention

We keep enquiry and shipment records for as long as we need them for the commercial relationship and for as long as applicable law requires us to retain them.

Your Choices

If you would like to know what information we hold about you, or would like it corrected or deleted, contact us at info@oceanuscontainer.com or on +971 4263 6272 and we will deal with your request.
