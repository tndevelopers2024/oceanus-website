Shipping Agency Representation

At Oceanus Line & Container Logistics LLC, we provide comprehensive Shipping Agency Representation services, acting as a reliable partner for tank operators, dry containers and reefer operators. From port coordination, cargo handling and documentation up to last mile delivery, our expert team ensures smooth operations at every stage.

  • Port coordination, cargo handling and documentation through to last mile delivery.
  • Safe inventory management for principals, plus reload opportunities back to principal demanding areas.

Service Overview

Representation only works if the principal can stop watching. We handle safe inventory management on the principal's behalf and actively find reload opportunities back to principal demanding areas, so equipment does not sit idle at the wrong end of a trade lane. With a focus on efficiency, transparency and professionalism, we deliver seamless support to optimize your shipping experience.

Service Included

  • Port coordination
  • Cargo handling
  • Documentation management
  • Last mile delivery
  • Safe inventory management
  • Reload opportunity sourcing
