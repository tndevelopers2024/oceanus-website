Standard Tanks

Standard Tanks Built For Bulk Liquid Cargo

Standard Tanks are T11 tank containers designed for the transportation of food grade, hazardous and non-hazardous cargoes, available in a wide range of capacities and can also be used as statutory storage tanks.

Technical Specifications

T11 ISO Tank
External Length 20 ft (6,058 mm)
External Width 8 ft (2,438 mm)
External Height 8 ft 6 in (2,591 mm)
Working Pressure (MAWP) Up to 4.0 Bar
Nominal Capacity 26,000 Litres
Tare Weight 3,540 – 3,830 kg
