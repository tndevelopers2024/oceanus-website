Project and Specialized Cargo Management

At Oceanus Line & Container Logistics LLC, we excel in Project and Specialized Cargo Management, offering customized solutions for the transportation of oversized, heavy or complex shipments. Our expertise covers end-to-end planning, route optimization and secure handling, ensuring your cargo reaches its destination safely and efficiently.

  • End-to-end planning, route optimization and secure handling.
  • Specialized equipment and experienced professionals for oversized and heavy lifts.

Service Overview

Project cargo does not fit the standard process, and treating it as though it does is how schedules slip. Every movement starts with a plan built around the actual dimensions, weight and route restrictions of the piece – not around a container. With specialized equipment, experienced professionals and a commitment to excellence, we deliver reliable and tailored logistics for industries worldwide.

Service Included

  • Oversized and heavy lift cargo
  • Route surveys and optimization
  • Specialized equipment sourcing
  • End-to-end movement planning
  • Secure handling and lashing
  • Port and terminal coordination

Key Benefits

End-to-End Planning

The full movement is planned before anything moves – dimensions, route restrictions, equipment and timing.

Route Optimization

Routes are selected around the real constraints of the cargo rather than the default lane.

Specialized Equipment

The right equipment for oversized, heavy or complex pieces, sourced for the specific movement.

Experienced Professionals

People who have handled this category of cargo before, at every stage of the journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What counts as project or specialized cargo?
    Oversized, heavy or complex shipments that cannot move as standard containerized freight – pieces that need specialized equipment, route planning and dedicated handling.
  • Do you plan the route in advance?
    Yes. Our expertise covers end-to-end planning and route optimization, so restrictions are identified before the cargo moves rather than during transit.
  • Which industries do you serve?
    We deliver reliable and tailored project logistics for industries worldwide, with particular depth in chemicals, petrochemicals and energy.
